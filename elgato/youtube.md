# Elgato Clips: YouTube house style

Learned 2026-09-28 from the public channel (read-only): **Elgato Clips**, handle `@ElgatoClips`, channel id
`UCJulpl_UqH3L6NTmxU9k24Q`. 100 videos listed; full metadata read for 24 of them (Jun–Sep 2026). Update this file
when the channel's style changes; the titles task reads it.

## What the channel posts
Short, single-topic clips from Elgato streams and shoots: a feature shown working, a buyer question answered, a
how-to, a comparison, a design story. Median length 70 s (12 s to 20 min); 37 of 100 are a minute or less, 20 are
over three minutes. Uploads were near-daily in September 2026. The channel already A/B-tests titles in Studio: some
videos' current titles differ from the ones they launched with.

## Titles
- Median 66 characters (middle half 61-72, max 98). Aim for 55-75, hard limit 100. Put the product and the payoff in
  the first ~50 characters; mobile cuts the rest.
- Title Case. Product names exactly as Elgato writes them: "Stream Deck +", "Stream Deck + XL", "Wave:3 MK.2",
  "Facecam MK.2", "4K X", "vs." with a period. Two-thirds of titles name "Elgato".
- The common shapes (count of 100):
  - `Elgato <Product>: <what it does for you>` (33): "Elgato Voice Focus: AI Noise Removal for Any Microphone",
    "Elgato Wave XLR Pro: Eliminate Desk Rumble with Low-Cut Filter"
  - `How to <do the thing> with/on Elgato <Product>` (12): "How to Copy and Paste Pages in Elgato Stream Deck"
  - A question (13): "Can You Use Stream Deck Mobile and Stream Deck at the SAME TIME? Multi-Device Setup",
    "Elgato 4K Pro/4K X: Do You Need an HDMI 2.1 Cable for VRR?"
  - `<A> vs. <B>: Which One Is Right for You?` (8): "Elgato Facecam MK.2 vs. Facecam 4K: Which Webcam Should You Buy?"
  - A pain-point opener (a few): "Stop Alt-Tabbing! Toggle OBS Studio Source Visibility with Elgato Stream Deck",
    "No Numpad? How to Use the GALLEON 100 SD Stream Deck as Your Custom Numpad"
  - Older livestream cut-downs end in `| Live from CES 2026` or `| Elgato Week Live Stream`.
- Occasional emphasis in capitals ("SAME TIME", "NO CODE", "EASY") is used sparingly: at most one word, and not in
  every title.
- For the A/B/C test, make the three titles genuinely different angles: e.g. A = product-led (`Elgato X: payoff`),
  B = the viewer's question or problem, C = the outcome or a how-to. A is what gets uploaded.

## Descriptions
Every recent description follows the same shape:

1. **Paragraph 1** (2 sentences): what the product/feature does for the viewer, naming it in full ("Elgato Stream
   Deck Pedal puts customizable actions at your feet..."). Sometimes ends with one relevant emoji.
2. **Paragraph 2** (2 sentences): what the clip actually shows or explains, and when it helps (streaming,
   recording, calls). Concrete, no hype, no invented specs or prices.
3. **Links**: one or two, either bare URLs or with a label ("Explore Stream Deck:", "Shop Facecam 4K:",
   "Download Camera Hub:"). Use only links from `elgato/links.json`.
4. Clips cut from a stream point to it: "Watch the full stream: <twitch link>" (Clip Scout adds this line).
5. The **Connect with Elgato** block below (Clip Scout adds it; don't write it yourself).
6. Optional: 3-6 hashtags on the last line (#StreamDeck #Elgato #StreamDeckPlus ...). About one in five videos uses them.

## Other settings
- Category: **People & Blogs** (`categoryId` 22) on every video checked, so Clip Scout uses 22 (not Science &
  Technology).
- Tags: none of the 24 videos checked use tags. Clip Scout suggests up to 8 short ones (product names, the feature);
  they're harmless and can be dropped.
- Playlists: the only public playlist is "CES 2026". Clip Scout suggests a playlist per topic (the Approved folder
  topics) but never creates playlists; missing ones are reported so someone can make them in Studio.
- Not made for kids; no altered or synthetic content.
